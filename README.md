# All of Statistics - Solved Exercises

As a part of my learning and with the goal to improve my understanding of statistics, I decided to start reviewing common concepts of Probability  and Statistics, which are, added with Linear Algebra and many others, at the core of Machine Learning and AI. The book ["All of Statistics"](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12SzAEbedqNqoZRXjYbA7IVkLXPmAOp0z/view?usp=sharing) by Larry A Wasserman is a great resource for this purpose and discusses a wide range of topics in Statistics and Probability that I studied in my  bachelor's degree in Mathematics. 

I give back to the community by sharing my solutions to the exercises in the book. As time will allow me, I will be adding solutions for more execrices from books that I have used in my studies and found useful and easy to understand..

The repository contains my solved exercices from the book ["All of Statistics"](https://drive.google.com/file/d/12SzAEbedqNqoZRXjYbA7IVkLXPmAOp0z/view?usp=sharing) by Larry A Wasserman. The solutions are written in latex, for the most of them, but some are handwritten and scanned as images.
